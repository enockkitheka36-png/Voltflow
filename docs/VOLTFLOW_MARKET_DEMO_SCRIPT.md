# Voltflow — Market Demo Script (11.6)

A 10-minute walkthrough for showing Voltflow to an EPC owner, sales lead or partner. **Internal Alpha — feasibility & workflow, not certified engineering.**

## 0 · Framing (30s)
"Voltflow takes a solar or water-pumping job from site survey → sizing → safety checks → BoQ → client proposal → delivery — with a readiness score and an advisor that tells you what's missing and whether it's safe to quote."

## 1 · Clean start (1m)
Open the app → **Welcome to Voltflow**. "No fake project, no clutter." Click **Try Demo Project → Off-grid Farm** (clearly badged DEMO).

## 2 · Dashboard as command centre (1.5m)
Point at the **readiness ring**, **critical blockers**, **next actions**, section cards. "In ten seconds you know how far along the job is and what's blocking a quote." Open the **🎯 Action Center** → every item has **Go fix →**.

## 3 · Technical trust: inverter/PV coherence (2m)
Open **Inverter + Safety**. If DC:AC is low, show the **"PV array is small compared to inverter"** card: "The app explains this inverter is **load-led**, the PV is below the ideal solar window, and gives options — add ~9 panels, or a smaller inverter if peak allows." Toggle the **diversity factor** and show the inverter respond. "This is the difference between a red number and a decision."

## 4 · Solar Borehole (2.5m)
Open **Solar Borehole** → **Enable**. Pick **Irrigation / Dairy farm**, **Estimate demand**, enter yield + depth. Show **Results**: litres/day → required flow → TDH → pump HP → **recommended PV & tank**. "We sell **water output and reliability**, not just kW. Storage is the battery." Show a **warning** (e.g. night pumping without a source) and the **confirm-borehole-test-report** note.

## 5 · Proposal + safety gate (1.5m)
Open **Proposal**. Show the **Solar Borehole section** + investment + assumptions. Click **Download PDF Quotation** with an open blocker → the **gate modal** warns with *Review first / Continue anyway*. "It won't silently issue a weak quote."

## 6 · Views (1m)
Switch **Client View** (clean, benefits, proposal) → **Technician View** (field checklist, borehole practical) → back to **Engineer**. "One project, three audiences."

## Close
"Everything is a **feasibility and workflow aid** — final engineering and borehole pump selection still need qualified review and the borehole test report. Today it's a hosted internal alpha; the roadmap is in the SaaS readiness doc."
